# Product discovery

## Purpose

Turn uncertain product questions into evidence-based recommendations and useful next steps.

## Use when

Use when the problem, audience, opportunity or best solution is unclear. Skip discovery ceremony when the user specifies a clear outcome.

## Workflow

1. Clarify audience, context, problem and desired outcome.
2. Inspect current product behavior and available analytics, feedback or research.
3. Separate observed evidence, assumptions, hypotheses and missing information.
4. Map the journey and identify friction, unmet needs and affected roles.
5. Compare options by user value, feasibility, risk and opportunity cost.
6. Identify the riskiest assumption and a proportionate validation method.
7. Recommend a direction and translate it into scope when asked.

## Rademan context

Use [current product context](../../../AI/PROJECT.md) and verify capabilities in the app. Rademan serves art discovery and artist–audience interaction; this summary is not a fixed boundary.

## Quality bar

Do not invent research, quotes, analytics or market facts. Distinguish evidence from interpretation and consider accessibility/localization. Prefer user outcomes over feature volume.

## Deliverable

A concise problem framing, evidence, opportunity, options, recommendation, assumptions and practical next step.
