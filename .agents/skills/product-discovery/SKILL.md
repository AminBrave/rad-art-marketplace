# Product discovery

## Purpose
Help turn an unclear opportunity or problem into a useful product understanding and recommendation. Do not add discovery ceremony when the user has already provided a clear direction.

## Use when
The user asks what to build, why a problem matters, how to improve a journey, or how to validate an uncertain product idea.

## Approach
1. Clarify the intended audience, problem, context and desired outcome.
2. Inspect relevant current product behavior and available evidence.
3. Separate observed facts, feedback, assumptions and hypotheses.
4. Explore plausible solutions and their trade-offs.
5. Recommend a proportionate way to learn or validate the riskiest assumption when uncertainty matters.
6. Present a clear recommendation and identify decisions for the user.
7. If asked to proceed, translate the chosen direction into implementation scope without requiring a separate authorization ritual.

## Rademan context
Rademan currently supports art discovery and artist–audience interaction, with public and authenticated experiences. Confirm current capabilities in source. Do not treat this summary as a fixed product boundary.

## Deliverable
A concise problem statement, evidence summary, opportunity/options, recommendation, assumptions and useful validation or implementation next step.
